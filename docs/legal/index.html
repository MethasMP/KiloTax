Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much money can I claim on my tax?

For the 2026–27 tax year, the official ATO rate is 91 cents per business kilometre. You can claim up to 5,000 work kilometres per car per year without keeping a full 12-week logbook. That is a tax deduction of up to $4,550 per vehicle. KiloTax automatically tracks this 5,000 km cap for you.

2. Is the app free, and do I have to create an account?

Yes, tracking trips and exporting your records is 100% free. You do not need to create an account, enter a password, or sign up with your email. All your drives stay saved safely on your own phone.

3. How do I get my records to my accountant?

Whenever tax time comes around: go to the Tax Summary screen, tap Export Records, and send the clean CSV spreadsheet or PDF report straight to your accountant or bookkeeper via email or Apple Files.

4. Do I need to keep these records?

Under Australian tax law (ITAA 1997 Subdivision 28-H, Section 28-150 and Section 900-165), the ATO requires you to keep proof of how you calculated your kilometres for 5 years after lodging your tax return. We suggest saving a copy of your exported PDF or CSV to your iCloud Drive or email archive.

KiloTax (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to handling personal and telemetry information in strict compliance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) set out in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Sections 5.1.1 and 5.1.2 of the Apple App Store Review Guidelines.

1. Local-First Processing & Data Minimisation

In standard operation, KiloTax operates on a local-first basis. Travel records, odometer logs, vehicle details, and deduction calculations remain within the application sandbox on the user's device. We do not sell, rent, commercialise, or harvest personal travel records.

2. Location Data, Hardware Telemetry & Apple ATT Compliance

When automated trip tracking is activated by the user, the application requests access to location hardware strictly to measure distance and generate verifiable travel records.

Zero Cross-App Tracking Warranty (Apple Guideline 5.1.2): KiloTax does not use third-party advertising identifiers (IDFA), data brokers, or cross-app behavioural trackers. KiloTax does not track users across third-party apps or websites under the Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. Telemetry data is never shared with advertising networks or motor insurance underwriters.

3. Camera & Receipt Processing

Where the user chooses to attach expense receipts or photograph odometer values, the application accesses device camera sensors solely to capture substantiated records. Receipt text recognition occurs on-device or within encrypted transient processing sandboxes.

4. User Rights & Permanent Account Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

Users maintain complete ownership over their data. Device-stored data can be purged by clearing local application data or uninstalling the software. Where optional cloud sync is enabled, users may request permanent account deletion directly in the app via Settings → Account → Delete Account & All Data, which permanently purges all records from servers within 30 days. Users may also email methaspak@gmail.com for manual deletion assistance.

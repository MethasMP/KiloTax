## 2. Privacy by Design: Local-First Principle
In our base and manual tracking modes (including the Cents per Kilometre / CPK log), KiloTax operates on a **Local-First Architecture**:
- All trip entries, odometer readings, vehicle details, and tax calculations are stored directly in your device’s local sandbox storage (`SQLite` / secure preferences).
- We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest your personal records.
- You can use the core features of the App without creating an account or transmitting personal data to our servers.

---

## 3. Information We Collect and Why

### A. Location Data (GPS & Telemetry)
- **Purpose:** Used strictly to record trip distances, origin and destination points, and create Australian Taxation Office (ATO) compliant vehicle travel records.
- **Background Location:** If you explicitly enable automated trip tracking, the App requests permission to access location data in the background (even when the App is closed or not in use) solely to detect when a vehicle drive begins and ends.
- **Limitation:** Telemetry data is never shared with third-party advertisers, insurance companies, or data brokers.
- **Control:** You may disable background location tracking at any time via your device’s operating system settings. The App degrades gracefully to manual distance and odometer entry.

### B. Device Storage & Camera / Photos
- **Purpose:** If you choose to attach odometer verification photos or fuel and maintenance expense receipts, the App accesses your camera or photo library.
- **Processing:** Receipt scanning and OCR text recognition occur on-device or within an encrypted transient processing sandbox. Images are stored in your secure vault.

### C. Account Information (Optional Cloud Vault & Sync)
- **Purpose:** If you activate cloud backup, subscription features, or multi-device sync, we collect minimal identifiers (such as your email address, Apple ID token, or Google authentication token).
- **Security:** Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3, and data stored in our cloud vault is protected by AES-256 encryption.

---

## 4. Statutory ATO 5-Year Record Retention
Under Australian taxation law (including *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997* Subdivision 28-F), taxpayers claiming motor vehicle deductions must maintain evidentiary records for **5 years** from the date of lodging the relevant tax return.

- KiloTax provides tools to export your complete records in standard open formats (`CSV` and `PDF`).
- We strongly recommend exporting your records and backing them up securely to your own personal storage or providing them to your registered tax agent.

---

## 5. Data Deletion & User Rights (Apple 5.1.1(v) Compliant)
You retain absolute sovereignty over your data:
- **Local Data:** You may clear local records at any time by resetting app data or uninstalling the App.
- **Account Deletion:** If you have created a cloud account, you may request instantaneous and permanent deletion directly in the App via:  
  **Settings → Account → Delete Account & All Data**.
- **Permanent Purge:** Upon deletion request, all associated trips, receipts, cloud backups, and credentials are permanently purged from active systems within 30 days.

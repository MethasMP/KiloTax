## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What is the ATO Cents per Kilometre rate for 2026–27?
For the 2026–27 income year, the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) statutory rate is **91 cents per business kilometre**.  
- You can claim up to a maximum of **5,000 business kilometres** per vehicle per year without needing a 12-week logbook.
- This represents a maximum allowable deduction of **$4,550** per eligible car.
- KiloTax automatically tracks your 5,000 km cap so you always know where you stand.

### 2. Do I need an account to use KiloTax?
No. In our standard mode, KiloTax operates completely local-first. All your trip entries, vehicle details, and calculations stay safely stored on your own iPhone. No sign-up or login is required.

### 3. How do I back up or export my tax records?
1. Open the **Tax Summary** tab in the App.
2. Tap **"Export Records"**.
3. Choose either **CSV (Spreadsheet)** or **Compliance Dossier (PDF)**.
4. Share it directly to your email, save to Apple Files / iCloud Drive, or send it straight to your accountant or bookkeeper.

### 4. How long does the ATO require me to keep my records?
Under Australian tax law, written evidence for motor vehicle claims must be retained for **5 years** starting from the date you lodge your tax return. We recommend saving an exported copy of your annual CSV/PDF to your personal cloud drive or filing cabinet.

### 5. What is the difference between Cents per Km (CPK) and the Logbook Method?
- **Cents per Km (CPK):** Simple flat rate (91c/km for 2026-27), capped at 5,000 km ($4,550 max claim).
- **Logbook Method:** Requires a continuous 12-week logbook to establish your business-use percentage, but allows you to claim your *actual* car running expenses (fuel, registration, insurance, repairs, servicing, and car depreciation) with **no 5,000 km cap**. KiloTax includes a side-by-side comparator so you can see which method yields the bigger deduction for your situation.

### 6. How do I delete my data or account?
Because KiloTax is local-first, you can clear all data at any time directly on your device. If you enabled cloud features, navigate to **Settings → Account → Delete Account & All Data** to permanently purge all data from our servers in compliance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v).

# Receipt workflow review — 10 November 2026

Status: **APPROVED for presenting the design proposal**, following independent Arbiter review. User approval of this concrete design is still required before product implementation. No product implementation or new test files in this review.

## Understanding lock

The user explicitly requested review beyond OCR, learning from competitors, then confirmed the priorities: accurate correction, ATO-aligned records, and substantially better UI/design. Existing PRODUCT.md establishes Australian sole traders/tradies, local-first capture, trustworthy evidence, correction/recovery, and accountant handoff. These requirements are locked for this review. The user explicitly selected the recommended end-to-end workflow option. This confirms scope; the concrete design still requires user approval before product implementation.

Success means the user can retain a bill, compare the actual image with extracted values, correct uncertainties without invented data, preserve the right vehicle context, and hand over retrievable evidence with honest review status. OCR accuracy and a human confirmation button alone do not establish tax deductibility or ATO acceptance.

## Research used

- [Dext mobile capture](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/105670-how-to-scan-and-upload-documents-in-the-dext-mobile-app): separate Single, Multiple and Combine modes; pre-submit image review and processing visibility. Lesson: document grouping is an explicit user choice.
- [Dext duplicate handling](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/216124-how-dext-handles-duplicate-cost-documents): exact-file rejection differs from detecting another file for the same purchase; review mode retains suspected duplicates. Lesson: distinguish identity from similarity and keep correction possible.
- [Hubdoc document statuses](https://central.xero.com/0/article/About-a-document-s-status-in-Hubdoc): Processing, Review and Failed are separate states. Lesson: extraction completion is not the end of the workflow.
- [Expensify scan recovery](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/reports-and-expenses/Troubleshoot-SmartScan-Issues): missing-field failures are actionable; replacement and manual correction are supported. Its documented processing needs connectivity; this is not a reason to discard KiloClaim's local-first constraint.
- [Expensify potential duplicates](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/reports-and-expenses/How-to-Find-and-Resolve-Flagged-Duplicate-Expenses): suspected matches remain resolvable by the user, including retaining separate purchases.
- [NN/g form error guidance](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/errors-forms-design-guidelines/): put corrective messages with the relevant fields, avoid relying on summary banners/tooltips and use interrupting modals sparingly.

### ATO requirements to model, not a universal compliance badge

Record capture, income-tax treatment and GST credits are distinct. Keep supplier, purchase/document dates where applicable, description, amount and supporting evidence/provenance; preserve how business use is determined. ATO's [business-record guidance](https://www.ato.gov.au/api/public/content/0-0e812e63-ce80-4a47-84e1-6013bc1393c6) includes expense records and mixed business/private use. Employee [work-expense guidance](https://www.ato.gov.au/api/public/content/0-5bb183ff-ac74-4e31-b91e-c147e6a387d3) corroborates receipt fields but is not treated as the complete sole-trader ruleset.

For GST, a qualifying tax invoice is generally needed for purchases over A$82.50 including GST, with exceptions and eligibility conditions. Invoice details include supplier identity/ABN, issue date, description, taxable extent and GST; buyer identity or ABN is additionally needed for sales of A$1,000 or more. See [ATO tax invoices](https://www.ato.gov.au/api/public/content/0-1e92db95-a75c-4f4e-a3d4-39f43b1a3b25) and [GST tax-invoice ruling](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?LocID=%22GST%2FGSTR20131%2FNAT%2FATO%2Ffp81%22&PiT=20231128000001). Mixed purchases may require an actual GST amount different from one-eleventh of total: [ATO mixed-purchase guidance](https://www.ato.gov.au/api/public/content/0-9fc804ad-a043-4a35-b540-16b71d9ca9bf). These conditions must not become a universal ABN/photo blocker for every income-tax record. GST registration/credit eligibility must not be inferred from scanning a bill.

For eligible cars, the cents/km rate already covers car running costs; retaining a fuel receipt does not authorize adding that cost again to an income-tax claim. [ATO car-expense guidance](https://www.ato.gov.au/api/public/content/fa6ae421-c51a-4fdc-8538-c90fd566bb85_TaxTimeToolkit_Carexpenses_pdf). This does not mean GST treatment is identical, and the car method must not be generalized to every commercial vehicle.

Some ATO page opens returned 403; the official indexed content and legal ruling were used. Before implementation, pin the applicable supported scenarios and source versions; this design is not an independent certification of the current tax engine.

## Alternatives and proposed direction

A. Cosmetic/scanner-only patch: cheapest, but leaves different single/batch save contracts and recovery gaps.

B. **Selected by the user:** repair the capture → review → recorded expense → evidence handoff workflow, using existing Flutter/native/local services and visible states. The concrete proposal is being peer-reviewed; scope selection is not treated as approval of an unseen design.

C. Full cloud accounting intake, bank feeds, email ingestion and payment workflows: wider cost/privacy/operational scope, deferred.

## Proposed decisions and acceptance boundaries

**D1 — A retained document precedes OCR and expense creation.** A durable document ID and readable local file reference must be acknowledged before showing Saved to device or starting extraction. Document states are persisted. OCR failure/back/restart retain a discoverable draft. One deterministic expense ID belongs to a document. A commit intent records its revision and proposed expense before write; expense persistence is awaited and replay/upsert is idempotent before the document is marked recorded. Verify interruptions after file write, extraction, expense write and completion. No false success, lost image reference or duplicate row is acceptable. Re-scans/results are revision-bound and cannot overwrite later manual edits.

**D2 — Pages and receipts are explicit units.** Single is the default; Multiple means independent bills; a multi-page bill remains one document with ordered pages and one total. Show thumbnails, page count and receipt count before commit; grouping must be correctable. Ambiguous or overlapping pages stay in review; do not add page totals together. No silent splitting, discarded repeats or automatic accounting entry per image. Sophisticated stitching/line-item accounting is outside this proposal.

**D3 — Model unknown values and correction provenance.** Purchase date, issue date, capture time and unknown date cannot share one fallback field. Preserve OCR observations and current user values separately, including date, currency and GST corrections. A default currency/category must be labelled as a suggestion, not an OCR fact. Persist these meanings across restart/export. Old records with unavailable provenance are legacy/unknown, not retroactively declared extracted or confirmed.

**D4 — Separate documents, expenses, evidence and estimates.** A captured draft does not enter the expense ledger or deduction estimate. Explicit record creation requires valid core facts; manual expenses without photos remain possible and carry missing-evidence/manual provenance. Human confirmation acknowledges reviewed facts only, not deductibility. Existing recorded expenses/totals remain unchanged on upgrade; do not silently migrate them into drafts. B1 specifies the authorized new-record consumer gates and exact method/allocation semantics; this is not a prohibition on fixing the new flow. Display recorded amounts and outstanding evidence counts honestly, with any estimate retaining its existing, separately audited policy. A draft tax pack of recorded expenses may include unresolved review items; B2 explicitly excludes all uncommitted receipt-inbox drafts. No OCR-confidence threshold substitutes for human review.

**D5 — Rules must be scenario-specific and audited.** Both capture paths share the reviewed save/validation contract, but merely calling current helpers is insufficient. Audit the GST fallback and auto-fix, field correction tracking and downstream exports. Missing GST remains unknown; mixed/non-taxable/unsupported cases are not forced to total/11. Separate income-tax car method from GST context; do not label ABN/checksum or receipt confirmation as compliance. Apply invoice requirements conditionally, preserving source/version and unresolved explanations. B1 specifies new-record consumer corrections permitted by the proposed scope; historical recorded values/totals are not silently rewritten, and no universal tax-engine certification is implied.

**D6 — Pin vehicle context; trip linkage is optional.** Persist the selected vehicle ID when capturing; show it in review rather than rereading primary vehicle at save. An explicit vehicle change clears/re-evaluates incompatible trip suggestions and revalidates method/year context. Deleted vehicles/trips cannot silently rebind a document. Validate links against latest data at commit. A suggested trip is not evidence of business purpose, and insurance/servicing does not need a trip merely to save its receipt.

**D7 — Retry and duplicate review are different.** Same-document retry reuses identity. Same file can point to an existing document; a different photo of the same suspected purchase is flagged, not deleted. Compare within the user's own record scope and keep legitimate repeat purchases resolvable. Do not use date/amount alone as proof. A pending duplicate must not produce two claimed expenses by an unnoticed retry.

**D8 — Readability and privacy are explicit constraints.** File existence/hash and small file size do not establish readable evidence. Check actual dimensions/content after image processing, retain a readable evidence copy and allow retake. Preserve content necessary for records, remove unnecessary sensitive metadata, and never present local storage as encrypted without verifying it. No cloud OCR, background upload or new recurring cost is introduced by this proposal. Serial bounded processing uses the current capture cap rather than copying competitors' larger limits; lifecycle owns recognizer disposal and per-item failure.

**D9 — Export evidence honestly.** Retain the active ZIP's real local bytes, receipt index, fingerprints and missing/changed-file issues. Scanned drafts and recorded expenses retain distinct statuses. A reference/path alone is not a recoverable photo or backup; do not invent public cloud URLs. Legacy dead export paths must not accidentally become the redesigned screen's destination.

**D10 — UI prioritizes comparison and next action.** Capture page: one camera action, secondary gallery/manual, visible saved-document count and explicit single/multiple meaning. Review page: document position (2 of 5), expandable readable image, then supplier/date/total/description; each uncertain field has a nearby explanation. Vehicle/business context and conditional GST details are secondary sections. Use persistent labels, meaningful numeric/date input, text plus icon for errors, reserved footer space, and existing theme/tap-size/type-scale constraints. Main action records reviewed facts; secondary action saves a draft. Cancel clearly exits without deleting saved drafts. No percentage-confidence hero, decorative scanner animation, invented merchant or tax-approved badge. With the keyboard/image expanded, users must retain a way to compare evidence and correct the active field. UI polish is not a substitute for native usability testing.

## Constraint contracts added after Guardian review

**C1 / ownership.** A draft is device-only and carries an immutable owner scope (local guest collection or authenticated user ID) plus session epoch and document revision. Capture, OCR, commit and restore results must check that scope/epoch/revision before applying any mutation. Sign-out, account switch, reset and account deletion invalidate outstanding jobs; wipe cannot be undone by a late OCR or restore completion. Kept drafts remain in their old scope; guest/account reassignment requires an explicit user action, never an inferred ownership change on login. After a document becomes a recorded expense, existing enabled account sync/receipt-backup behavior may run for that same owner; the draft itself is not newly uploaded. D8 means no new cloud OCR/provider/draft-upload flow, not a false promise that existing configured expense sync is disabled. Show on-device versus account-backup state honestly. Sign-out keep/wipe and cross-account recovery are acceptance cases.

**C2 / shared writes.** The commit boundary includes document, expense and reciprocal trip state. All writers to these aggregates—including capture commit, manual edits, cloud-restore merge and replay—must apply mutations through one short app-level serialized write coordinator, using fresh state inside the critical section. OCR/network/image work stays outside it. The journal records intended revision, stable expense ID, context and completion, with acknowledged writes. Treat expense.linkedTripId as the canonical relationship; reconcile the trip's reciprocal expense IDs from canonical expenses on replay/startup, preserving unrelated trip fields. A stale edit or restore must not overwrite a newer local revision: retain the draft and surface a conflict for review. No reliance on separate unawaited whole-list preference writes. Interleaved two-document commit, duplicate retry, restore and failures between component writes must retain both expenses exactly once and reconcile links. Implementation may use existing storage plus a journal; a database rewrite is not mandated, but this contract cannot be claimed satisfied by UI-only changes.

**C3 / resource budget.** Proposed initial support limits, to validate on supported hardware before release: 20 pages per capture session, 20 MiB source bytes and 24 megapixels per page, 80 MiB aggregate imported bytes per session, and 250 MiB of outstanding app-managed draft image bytes. These are conservative design limits, not measured performance claims or ATO rules. Inspect file length and image header metadata before full decode; process one bounded working image at a time, with a proposed 2400 px longest-edge OCR working copy only if readability remains acceptable. Never stitch an unbounded composite bitmap or use the reduced copy as proof the original content is readable. Exceeding a limit stops accepting new input and explains the limit/retake path; it does not delete valid retained tax records. If initial copy fails because storage is full, the document must say image not saved, retain recoverable metadata/source where available and offer retry/recapture—never promise saved evidence that the filesystem could not write. Already saved drafts stay available. Allocation/retention of original and derived copies counts toward the draft budget; no automatic expiry silently destroys committed records. Export must stream receipt data/hashing/ZIP entries to staged output with bounded buffers, not hold a full financial year's receipt bytes in Archive memory. Share only a completed manifest/output; storage errors clean incomplete export files and retain source records. Validate large image, full disk, background/interruption and large-year export on minimum supported devices before performance sign-off. Native decoding/OCR format support must be verified; WebP compatibility cannot be assumed merely because compression succeeds.

## Supported-scenario boundary for implementation planning

The proposed first implementation improves receipt facts and evidence workflows for the existing Australian expense categories. AUD receipts can be reviewed for the existing income-tax methods only when the vehicle/method is applicable; do not infer method eligibility from the word ute. Capture can retain other currencies, refunds/credit notes, unusual invoices or unsupported GST circumstances as review-required documents, but must not force a positive AUD expense, invent an exchange rate or silently turn a refund into a purchase. This proposal does not implement BAS filing, GST-credit entitlement, invoice payment, capital-asset treatment or a new tax calculation engine. Record the actual printed GST or unknown; never display a computed GST credit solely from a scan. Any affected estimate/export helper must be audited against the declared scenario before it consumes new records. Existing records and historical totals are not silently rewritten. Drafts are excluded; new records must have explicit use/context rather than inheriting 100% business use. This boundary is a scoped engineering proposal, not an assertion every existing tax formula is already correct.

## Builder-check resolutions

**B1 / downstream semantics for new records.** The new flow must write an explicit versioned treatment/provenance state; it may not feed a new correctly captured personal/unknown expense into old consumers as an implicitly 100%-business record. Product implementation is authorized to change consumers for these new-version records and their exports, while preserving historical v1 recorded values/totals and explicitly marking legacy review limitations. It is not authorized to certify or silently recalculate legacy tax treatment. Recorded total, evidence status and supported deduction estimate are separate outputs.

| New-record state/context | Ledger / accountant data | Additional income-tax estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Captured/uncommitted document draft | Inbox only; not an expense | Excluded |
| Valid recorded facts, unresolved tax/GST basis or unsupported category/circumstances | Record facts and review reason; leave calculated claim fields unresolved, not falsely zero | Excluded from a ready estimate; outstanding-value/count is visible |
| Unrelated personal purchase | Recorded facts, explicitly personal | Zero; no business deduction inferred |
| Eligible-car cents/km running-cost receipt | Record-only evidence with explicit method reason | Excluded as an additional running-cost deduction; no inference about GST-credit entitlement |
| Supported logbook running cost for that vehicle, with valid applicable logbook share and resolved tax cost basis | Actual receipt total stays unchanged; separate vehicle-attributable/tax cost basis and provenance | Tax cost basis attributable to the vehicle × applicable logbook share, once |
| Supported direct business toll/parking cost with explicit use and resolved tax basis | Retain actual total and explicit allocation | Supported tax basis × explicit business proportion, once |
| Capital/tool/equipment, mixed items or other treatment not resolved by a supported rule | Record-only, needs tax review | No guessed immediate deduction |

For logbook running costs, do not treat a per-receipt 'For work' selection as 100% business use or multiply a second work-use percentage by the logbook percentage. The UI asks whether the cost belongs to the selected vehicle; if the bill includes other vehicles/items, record the attributable amount only from an explicit, supportable allocation and leave unresolved treatment for review. The generic business-use percentage control applies only where the supported expense rule actually uses that allocation. The prototype's vehicle-service example follows this distinction. Logbook support follows [ATO car-expense guidance](https://www.ato.gov.au/api/public/content/0-f47d56cc-0aae-429d-9b35-38375943b40c).

Unknown printed GST is nullable, not manufactured as total/11 and not treated as an asserted zero credit. The receipt's GST amount and the GST credit the taxpayer is entitled to recover are different facts. Where GST recovery can affect income-tax cost basis, keep treatment unresolved until that basis is established; this scope does not calculate GST-credit entitlement. Known non-GST-registered cases can use their supported gross cost basis; otherwise do not guess gross versus net merely from an ABN or invoice. [ATO explains the general reduction of deductible expenditure by input-tax credits that can be claimed](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?LocID=%22SAV%2FDEPRECIATING%2FATROLLOVERRELIEF%22&PiT=20251029000001). New estimate/export consumers must use the explicit treatment gate and actual/unknown GST data, not the legacy unconditional effectiveGst fallback. Example acceptance: an eligible running-cost basis of 100 with a validated logbook share of 60% contributes 60 once, not 100 or 36; an unrelated personal cost contributes zero; an unresolved cost has a blank/unresolved claim value with a review reason, not a fabricated result. These are engineering examples, not a determination of a real purchase's eligibility.

**B2 / accountant-export population.** The active vehicle/FY ZIP includes recorded expenses (including recorded expenses with evidence-review issues) under its existing scope rules, with actual receipt bytes where available. It excludes all uncommitted document drafts. 'Draft tax pack' means a pack of recorded facts that still has review issues; it does not mean receipt-inbox drafts are silently assigned to a vehicle or year. Unknown purchase dates stay in the owner-scoped inbox, not an invented FY. Before export, disclose that unrecorded drafts are not included and link back to the inbox; do not claim a complete receipt collection while omitting them silently. Keep their image/data retained. A new document-only/accountant intake export is outside this scope.

## User interaction contracts added after Advocate review

**U1 / action meanings.** Missing supplier, purchase date, description or a finite positive amount prevents Record expense, with the reason at that field; Save draft remains available once the image/data is durably retained. A new bill with unresolved applicable cost context stays a draft rather than inheriting the model's 100% business default. For logbook running costs, review vehicle attribution and use the logbook share separately; any explicit allocation must match the supported rule in B1. A manually entered expense with valid core facts and explicit use may be recorded without a photo, labelled evidence incomplete; photo/ABN is not a universal blocker. Unknown or unsupported GST is kept unknown and excluded from any GST-credit assertion; it does not prevent retaining the document or recording an otherwise valid income-tax expense. Conditional issues may leave the record needing further evidence review. Human facts confirmation is never ATO approval. Completion language distinguishes Saved to device · needs review, Expense recorded · evidence incomplete, and Details reviewed · tax treatment may need review. Drafts do not enter expense/estimate totals; existing historical treatment is not silently rewritten.

**U2 / small-screen comparison.** The visual prototype must show 390 and 320 px widths, 100% and 200% text, and a keyboard-height layout study. At constrained height, the full preview collapses to a sticky View receipt rail; opening it preserves all edits, scroll position and the active field and returns focus to that field. Persistent labels wrap rather than truncate. The form scrolls independently above a reserved footer; actions may stack and must not overlap content. A simulated HTML keyboard is only a layout study; Flutter/native keyboard, focus, screen-reader and real-device behavior remain acceptance checks before shipping.

**U3 / counting and grouping.** Say Receipt 2 of 3 · 2 pages, and separately show 3 receipts · 4 photos in the queue. Capture modes explain whether photos represent independent purchases or pages of one bill. A grouping view shows each page thumbnail and allows explicit merge/split before any expense is recorded; grouping changes neither discard images nor add page totals. Returning to review preserves per-document corrections. Ambiguous grouping stays a retained draft.

**U4 / duplicate outcomes.** Show the existing recorded expense and new captured document together, including image, supplier, date and total; existing user-corrected values take precedence over its old OCR observations. Use existing record links the capture to that expense and creates no second expense. These are separate purchases retains each document and requires normal review before recording the new purchase. Decide later saves a pending-duplicate draft with no additional expense. No silent merge, replacement or deletion is proposed.

**U5 / conditional detail disclosure.** Keep supplier/date/description/total as the main task. Expand the particular context section only when an applicable issue needs action: business-use allocation, vehicle changed, or requested GST review requiring invoice information. Optional trip matching stays optional. Show GST shown on receipt separately from GST-credit eligibility; no total/11 autofill and no universal ABN failure. Generic warnings may summarize counts but must link to the actual field and not replace its local explanation. Do not add decorative status cards that compete with the main correction task.

## Supabase and mixed-receipt follow-up — 10 November 2026

Read-only inspection of connected KiloClaim project `mwtxfdqcyalsohruzmps` confirmed that deployed `expenses` stores one amount/category, supplier, date, notes, receipt_storage_path and business_percentage, with vehicle/trip/user links. It has no line-item allocation structure. The receipts bucket is private. Separate `audit_evidence` metadata includes storage_path, image_sha256, captured_at and capture_source; this does not establish that every expense is linked to such a record.

Material client/schema mismatch: `sync_engine_service.dart` sends `gst_amount` and `sha256_hash` to expenses, but neither column exists in the inspected live table. It omits the existing supplier column and local receiptAudit. The expense HTTP non-2xx path does not make the overall SyncResult.success false.

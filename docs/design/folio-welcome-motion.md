# Folio welcome motion

User-approved scope: one-shot Folio assembly on Welcome. Sign-in, Home, Settings, launcher and native launch marks stay static.

## Design contract

- Reuse the raw existing 800ms Welcome controller. No second controller/ticker.
- Front/lower plane starts at 0ms, middle at 80ms, back/upper at 160ms.
- Each plane eases out over 400ms: opacity 0→1, vertical offset 6→0 logical pixels. All finish by 560ms.
- Preserve source SVG stacking order, colour and geometry. Keep the 44px slot, gap and wordmark stationary.
- Get started and Sign in are available immediately.
- Reduce Motion shows the finished mark with no animation. Turning it on mid-flight settles the controller; turning it off does not replay.
- Rebuilding Welcome does not replay. A fresh Welcome mount may play once again.

## Completion gates

Behaviour tests cover immediate action, timing, Reduce Motion changes, no replay, disposal, static defaults and stable text/layout. Completed canvas artwork is compared against the production SVG. Normal and 200% text captures must remain usable. Independent review must have no unresolved actionable findings. Preview captures are widget renders, not recordings from a physical device.
